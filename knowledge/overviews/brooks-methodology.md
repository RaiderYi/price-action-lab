# Brooks Methodology Overview

## One-sentence Definition
Al Brooks' complete Price Action trading methodology -- analyzing market structure through naked price charts, identifying high-probability entry signals, and managing risk.

## Core Philosophy

### 1. Price Action = Everything
> "Price action is any representation of price movement of any financial instrument on any type of chart and any time frame." -- Al Brooks

- No indicators, only price itself
- All information reflected in price and volume
- See: [[price-action-definition]]

### 2. Three Market States

| State | Characteristics | Strategy |
|-------|-----------------|----------|
| **Trend** | HH/HL or LL/LH | With-trend, pullback entries |
| **Range** | Oscillating between S/R | Counter-trend at extremes |
| **Transition** | Trend->Range or vice versa | Wait for confirmation |

See: [[fsm-market-cycle]], [[channel-to-trading-range]], [[breakout-mode]]

### 3. 80/20 Rule
- In trends: 80% of reversal attempts fail
- In ranges: 80% of breakouts fail

See: [[eighty-percent-rule]], [[forty-sixty-rule]]
