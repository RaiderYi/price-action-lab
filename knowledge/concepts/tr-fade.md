# TR Fade

## One-sentence Definition
TR Fade is a counter-trend entry at trading range extremes after multiple tests (typically 3rd or 4th test), betting on the 80% failure rate of range breakouts.

## Core Points
- Enter counter-trend at range extreme after 3-4 tests
- 80% of breakout attempts from ranges fail
- Need range quality confirmation (sufficient bars, clear boundaries)
- Often combined with wedge or double-top/bottom patterns
- Risk/reward favorable when range is wide enough
