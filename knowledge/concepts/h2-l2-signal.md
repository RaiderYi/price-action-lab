# H2/L2 Signal

## One-sentence Definition
H2 (second Higher low in bull trend) and L2 (second Lower high in bear trend) are high-probability trend-continuation signals after a two-legged pullback.

## Core Points
- H2: Two-legged pullback to EMA or support, then resumption up
- L2: Two-legged pullback to EMA or resistance, then resumption down
- One of the most reliable Brooks signals
- Must have proper close position (upper half for H2, lower half for L2)
- Works in trends and sometimes at range extremes
