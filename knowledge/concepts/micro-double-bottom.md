# Micro Double Bottom

## One-sentence Definition
A micro double bottom is a small-scale double bottom pattern where two lows are within 0.5 ATR of each other, serving as a frequent scalp entry signal.

## Core Points
- Two lows within 0.5 ATR -- essentially an H2 variant
- Most frequent scalp signal in the system
- Often called "Horn" pattern by Brooks
- Works best in trends and at range extremes
- Requires confirmation bar with strong close
