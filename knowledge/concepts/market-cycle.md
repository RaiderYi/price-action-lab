# Market Cycle

## One-sentence Definition
The market cycle describes the recurring progression of market states: breakout -> channel -> trading range -> new breakout, forming a continuous loop of price action.

## Core Points
- Breakout: price escapes a range with momentum
- Channel: sustained directional movement with pullbacks
- Trading Range: equilibrium between buyers and sellers
- Transition: ambiguous state between clear structures
- Cycle repeats on all timeframes (fractal nature)
