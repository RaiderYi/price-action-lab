# Wedge Signal

## One-sentence Definition
A wedge is a three-push reversal pattern where each successive push is smaller, converging toward a point -- indicating exhaustion and high-probability reversal.

## Core Points
- Three pushes in same direction with converging trend lines
- Each push weaker than previous (smaller bars, less momentum)
- Third push often fails to reach trend line = strongest signal
- Can be broad or tight; broad wedges more reliable
- Works on all timeframes and in all market types
