# SPB (Strong Pullback)

## One-sentence Definition
SPB (Strong Pull Back) is a deep but controlled counter-trend move that creates high-probability limit order entries at EMA or prior support/resistance levels.

## Core Points
- Deeper than normal pullback but not a trend reversal
- Price quickly returns to EMA or key level
- Best entry: limit orders at EMA or H1/H2 levels
- Often occurs after news events or opening volatility
- Indicates strong trend conviction despite temporary counter-move
