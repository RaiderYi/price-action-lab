# Final Flag

## One-sentence Definition
A final flag is a small trading range that forms late in a trend, representing the last pause before the trend reverses or accelerates into a climax.

## Core Points
- Occurs after a strong trend has been underway for many bars
- Often a 3-5 bar consolidation with small range
- Breakout from final flag usually fails quickly
- One of the best reversal setups when combined with other signals
- Frequently seen after measured moves
