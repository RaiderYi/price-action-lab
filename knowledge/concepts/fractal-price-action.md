# Fractal Price Action

## One-sentence Definition
Price action patterns repeat at all timeframes and scales -- a 5-minute chart shows the same structures as a daily chart, demonstrating the self-similar (fractal) nature of markets.

## Core Points
- Same patterns appear on 1-minute, 5-minute, hourly, daily charts
- Higher timeframe (HTF) structure dominates lower timeframe (LTF)
- Multi-timeframe analysis confirms or invalidates setups
- Fractal nature means skills transfer across timeframes
- Key principle: always check HTF before taking LTF entry
