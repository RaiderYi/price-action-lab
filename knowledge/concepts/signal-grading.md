# Signal Grading

## One-sentence Definition
Signal grading is the process of evaluating trade setup quality by scoring multiple factors (bar strength, context, location, confluence) to filter out low-probability entries.

## Core Points
- Multiple dimensions: bar close, overlap, trend alignment, location
- Higher grades = higher probability + better risk/reward
- Used to determine position size and confidence
- Integrated into the RS scoring system
- Dynamic grading adjusts with market conditions
