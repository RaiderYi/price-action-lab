# Measured Move

## One-sentence Definition
A measured move is a price projection technique where the distance of the first leg of a trend is projected from the end of the pullback to estimate the target for the second leg.

## Core Points
- First leg distance = second leg target (roughly)
- Used for profit targets and assessing trend maturity
- 100% measured move is common; 50% and 150% also occur
- When price reaches measured move target, trend may reverse or pause
- Can be applied to trends, ranges, and reversals
