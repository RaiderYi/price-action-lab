# Post-Climax Reversal

## One-sentence Definition
A post-climax reversal occurs after an extreme price move (climax bar >1.8 ATR), when the market exhausts and reverses after completing a TBTL (Ten Bars Two Legs) pattern.

## Core Points
- Requires climax bar with large range (>1.8 ATR typically)
- TBTL confirmation: 10 bars and two legs of pullback
- One of the strongest reversal signals
- Often marks the end of a trend or major swing
- Requires patience -- don't enter before TBTL completes
