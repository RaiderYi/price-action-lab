# Double Pattern

## One-sentence Definition
A double pattern (double top/bottom, double wedge, etc.) occurs when price tests a level twice, creating a higher-probability setup than single tests.

## Core Points
- Double top: two highs at similar price, second often lower
- Double bottom: two lows at similar price, second often higher
- Second test has higher probability of reversal
- Works in trends (pullback entries) and ranges (extreme reversals)
- Often combined with other signals for confirmation
