# Wedge Flag

## One-sentence Definition
A wedge flag is a corrective pattern within a trend where the pullback takes wedge shape (three pushes against trend), followed by trend resumption.

## Core Points
- Three-push pullback in counter-trend direction
- Each push smaller than previous
- Breakout in trend direction resumes original move
- Often occurs mid-trend, not at extremes
- Distinguish from reversal wedge: context and location matter
