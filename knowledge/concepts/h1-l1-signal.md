# H1/L1 Signal

## One-sentence Definition
H1 (first Higher high in bull trend) and L1 (first Lower low in bear trend) are the earliest trend-continuation signals, occurring when the first pullback fails to break the prior extreme.

## Core Points
- H1: In bull trend, first bar with higher high after a pullback
- L1: In bear trend, first bar with lower low after a pullback
- Simplest and most frequent trend-continuation setup
- Lower probability than H2/L2 but earlier entry
- Often used for scaling into positions
