# Channel

## One-sentence Definition
A channel is a trend where the price oscillates between two parallel trend lines -- a sign of sustained directional movement with periodic pullbacks.

## Core Points
- Channels appear in trends: bull channels (higher highs and higher lows) and bear channels (lower highs and lower lows)
- 70% rule: ~70% of breakout attempts from a channel fail and return to the channel
- Tight channel: strong trend, hard to enter
- Broad channel: weaker trend, more trading opportunities
- Channel evolution: often transitions to trading range or breaks out
